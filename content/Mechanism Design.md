# Introduction
Instead of taking the game as a given and analyzing its equilibria as is the case in [[Algorithmic Game Theory]], Mechanism Design involves engineering the rules of the game to achieve a specific objective, assuming players will act in their own self-interest. It can be considered as *reverse of AGT*

We are currently focusing on designing [[Auctions]]
