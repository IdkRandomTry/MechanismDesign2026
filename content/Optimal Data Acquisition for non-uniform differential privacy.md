# Central Idea
## Problem Statement
How should a platform optimally buy data from privacy-sensitive users when users have different privacy concerns and can strategically misreport them for higher compensation?

## Optimal Estimation with Differential Privacy
How should the platform calculate an estimate (of the mean in our case) while ensuring [[Differential Privacy (DP)]] to the users in both central and local settings. Check out [[(Near) Optimal Estimation with DP]].

## Data Acquisition with Privacy Design
How to design incentives ([[Mechanism Design]]) to ensure users donot misreport their cost to privacy. Check out [[Platforms Objective]] and [[Data Acquisition with Privacy]].
